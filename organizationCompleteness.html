Completeness by client SHARE · Prototype

Each column scores a sample of the organization’s ROR-identified records in one client, as those records are today. The All of DataCite column is the same population with no client filter — the ecosystem reference. Because the query selects records that already carry the ROR, these scores describe the well-identified subset of the organization’s output; clients differ mostly because their forms and pipelines differ, not because one institution’s researchers are better documentarians. Thin columns (fewer than 10 records) — hollow points in Lines, dimmed cells in Grid — have noisy percentages.
